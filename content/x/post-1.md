When an agency account manager resigns, years of context walk out the door: what the client hates, what failed, who approves what.

The new person repeats old mistakes.

We built ContextRelay to fix this:
- Client-isolated long-term memory via Hindsight
- Temporal awareness (tracks when decisions evolve)
- Strictly grounded answers with verifiable quotes & timestamps
- Automated Handover Dossiers for incoming account managers

The person can leave. The client context should not.

Architecture & code: https://github.com/Shezan-op/context-relay
